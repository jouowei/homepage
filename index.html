01

論點The thesis

大部分人問的是「這家公司會不會賺錢」。我習慣往前站一步問： 這條路上，哪一段是繞不過去的？

Most people ask whether a company will make money. I have a habit of standing one step further back and asking: on this road, which stretch can nobody route around?

這個習慣不是從財報學來的，是從冷鏈車學來的。訂單再漂亮，車不夠就是出不去。 所以「瓶頸」對我從來不是比喻，那是我每天早上要調度的東西。

I did not pick this habit up from financial statements. I picked it up from refrigerated trucks. However good the orders look, if you are short a truck the goods do not move. So a bottleneck has never been a metaphor to me. It is the thing I dispatch every morning.

後來我發現，看資本市場其實是同一回事。錢可以在一週內決定要蓋多少座資料中心， 但電力、冷卻、材料、航道和產能不會跟著加速。故事跑得多快，最後是物理說了算。

It took me a while to notice that capital markets work the same way. Money can decide how many data centres to build in a week. Power, cooling, materials, shipping lanes and capacity do not speed up to match. However fast the story runs, physics has the last word.

所以我找的是那種稀缺、不容易被稀釋、剛好卡在必經之路上的東西， 並且盡量在信用大舉湧入、把它定價完成之前就先待在那裡。

So I look for things that are scarce, hard to dilute, and sitting on a path everyone has to take. And I try to already be there before the credit floods in and finishes pricing it.

然後，每一個部位我都會先寫下一句話：什麼證據出現，我就承認自己看錯了。 還會給它一個日期。

Then, for every position, I write one sentence in advance: what evidence would make me admit I was wrong. And I give that sentence a date.

02

三條看起來不相干的路 Three paths that look unrelated

我的履歷跳來跳去。但回頭看，它們解的是同一個問題。 My CV jumps around. Looking back, all three were solving the same problem.

Operator

營運者Operator

八年做軍公教通路和零售儲運，服務全聯、家福、捷盟、寶雅、Costco 這些通路。 我管的是實體世界的吞吐量：東西進得來嗎、出得去嗎、幾點到、溫度撐不撐得住。

Eight years in public-sector retail channels and distribution, serving PXMart, Carrefour, Jetmall, Poya and Costco. What I manage is throughput in the physical world: can it come in, can it go out, will it arrive on time, will the temperature hold.

這讓我對瓶頸的感覺很直接，不是從財報反推出來的想像。

That gives me a first-hand feel for bottlenecks, not one reverse-engineered from a filing.

Engineer

系統工程師Systems engineer

資訊管理碩士。做過製造和庫存的 ERP，和三位工程師在四個月內把一套 POS 系統生出來， 在 Appier 支援六百多個跨市場客戶，順手寫了些查 IP、加密、解析素材的小工具。

MSc in Management Information Systems. Built ERP modules for manufacturing and inventory, shipped a POS system in four months with three other engineers, supported 600+ clients across markets at Appier, and wrote small internal tools along the way for IP lookup, list encryption and creative parsing.

這讓我養成一個習慣：把流程寫成規格，把判斷寫成條件。

That left me with a habit: turn process into spec, turn judgement into conditions.

Allocator

配置者Allocator

用規則管理跨市場的證券組合：美股、台股、日股、指數期貨、選擇權。 分層配置，留一層現金地板，稅務效率會影響我選哪個工具進場。

A rules-based securities portfolio across markets: US, Taiwan and Japan equities, index futures, options. Tiered allocation, a cash floor kept underneath it, and tax efficiency shaped by which instrument I use to get in.

這裡我不太靠盤感。靠的是事先設好的閘門、觸發條件，和事後回頭對答案。

Very little of this runs on gut feel. It runs on gates set in advance, triggers, and going back afterwards to check.

03

我看世界的四層The four layers I read the world in

一則新聞進來，我第一件事是問它屬於哪一層。層跟層打架的時候，誰說了算。 When a piece of news arrives, my first question is which layer it belongs to. And when two layers disagree, which one wins.

  1. L0

    能量Energy

    電力、燃料、關鍵材料。這是其他所有層的預算上限，而且它變不出來。

    Power, fuel, critical materials. The budget ceiling for every other layer, and the one thing you cannot conjure.

  2. L1

    權力與信用Power and credit

    利率、期限溢酬、信用利差、財政、地緣。它決定錢從哪裡來、用什麼條件來。

    Rates, term premium, credit spreads, fiscal policy, geopolitics. This decides where the money comes from and on what terms.

  3. L2

    物理與技術Physics and technology

    產能、瓶頸、資本支出、供應鏈、航道。它決定錢最後變成什麼，以及變得多快。

    Capacity, bottlenecks, capex, supply chains, shipping lanes. This decides what the money turns into, and how fast.

  4. L3

    情緒與流動性Sentiment and liquidity

    價格、波動、部位、槓桿、故事。這是上面三層被市場定價之後的樣子，而定價常常是錯的。

    Prices, volatility, positioning, leverage, narrative. What the three layers above look like once the market has priced them, which it frequently gets wrong.

事先寫好的規則A rule written in advance

當 L2 的物理數據跟 L3 的市場情緒打架，物理優先。 這條我是事先寫死的，不是當下才決定。因為衝突發生的那個當下， 正好是我最不該相信自己心情的時候。

When the physical data in L2 disagrees with market sentiment in L3, physics wins. I fixed that rule ahead of time rather than deciding in the moment, because the moment of conflict is exactly when my mood is least worth trusting.

04

我借來的六副眼鏡Six lenses I borrowed

六個彼此獨立的框架。獨立這件事很重要：它們如果從不同方向指到同一個結論，那個結論才值得下注。 Six frameworks that are independent of each other. The independence is the point. When they arrive at the same conclusion from different directions, that conclusion is worth betting on.

康波週期Kondratiev waves

判斷我們站在長波的哪一段，以及舊波和新波重疊在哪裡。

Where we sit in the long wave, and where the old one overlaps the new.

佩蕾絲框架Carlota Perez

技術革命從安裝期走到部署期，資本的性格會整個換一副臉。

As a technological revolution moves from installation to deployment, capital changes personality entirely.

信用物理Credit as a fluid

信用是有物理限制的流體，不是無限的。要追蹤它從哪一層流到哪一層。

Credit is a fluid with physical limits, not an infinite one. The job is tracking which layer it flows from and into.

三浪模型Three waves

賽道的生命週期。知道自己站在第幾浪，比預測價格更能決定風險報酬。

The life cycle of a sector. Knowing which wave you are standing in shapes risk and reward more than any price forecast.

Marks 的鐘擺Howard Marks on cycles

市場情緒現在擺到哪一邊，以及擺回來要付多少代價。

Which way sentiment has swung, and what it will cost when it swings back.

結構人口學Structural demography

Turchin。菁英過度生產、財政壓力、民意張力，是政治風險的先行結構。

Turchin. Elite overproduction, fiscal stress and popular strain as the structure that runs ahead of political risk.

05

物理瓶頸圖譜The bottleneck atlas

系統裡我花最多時間維護的東西：一張 5 個層級乘 5 種信用流的表，25 格，每一格自己一個燈號。 The artefact I spend the most time maintaining: a table of 5 levels by 5 credit flows, 25 cells, each carrying its own status light.

每一格只回答一個問題：這裡現在是綠燈、黃燈、紅燈，還是黑燈。 這不是預測，是量測。所以它可以被推翻，這正是我要的。

Each cell answers one question: is this green, amber, red or black right now. That is a measurement, not a forecast. Which means it can be overturned, and that is the point.

真正有用的資訊其實不在單一格，而在燈號怎麼移動。 瓶頸一旦鬆開，收費站就不再是收費站；瓶頸換了位置，租金也跟著搬家。

The useful information is rarely in any single cell. It is in how the lights move. Once a bottleneck eases, the tollbooth stops being a tollbooth. When the bottleneck moves, the rent moves with it.

06

決策協議Decision protocols

判斷如果只是一次性的靈感，下次就複製不了。所以系統裡每一種分析都有名字，有固定的輸入、輸出，和固定要寫回哪裡。 A judgement that arrives as a one-off flash of insight cannot be repeated. So every kind of analysis in the system has a name, a fixed input, a fixed output, and a fixed place it gets written back to.

P

晨巡Morning sweep

每天固定掃一遍，把當日的座標基線立起來。

A fixed daily scan that sets the day's baseline coordinates.

A

新聞物理轉譯News to physics

把新聞的故事剝掉，還原成物理和信用的增量。大多數新聞的增量是零。

Strip the story off a headline and reduce it to physical and credit deltas. For most news the delta is zero.

T

物理偵察Physical recon

針對某個瓶頸做一手查證，不接受二手轉述。

First-hand verification on a specific bottleneck. No second-hand accounts accepted.

C

戰略校準Strategic recalibration

定期更新全域座標快照，並自動把下游該掃的東西叫起來。

Periodically refresh the global snapshot, and automatically wake whatever downstream scans it implies.

E

情境推演Scenario work

多條分支路徑和機率，每一條都要附上自己的否證條件。

Branching paths with probabilities, each one carrying its own condition for being ruled out.

G0

標的前篩Candidate screen

從瓶頸層級反推候選名單，而不是從熱門清單裡挑。

Work backwards from the bottleneck layer to a candidate list, rather than picking off a popular one.

F

個股盡調Deep dive

進場前的強制流程，包含管理層說法的偏差偵測。

A mandatory process before any position, including a check for bias in what management says.

M2

航海日誌Ship's log

收盤座標，和當天的決策留痕。

Closing coordinates, plus a trace of the day's decisions.

M3

對外翻譯Translating outward

用不帶術語的話講給外人聽，順便收集他們的反駁。

Explain it to an outsider without the jargon, and collect their objections while I am at it.

M4

對內回顧Looking back inward

對自己把來龍去脈重講一次。回顧、教學、稽核，不重做分析。

Retell the whole chain of reasoning to myself. Review, teach, audit. No redoing the analysis.

為什麼要這麼囉唆Why bother with all this

因為三個月後我想查得到：當時我知道什麼、我怎麼推的、我錯在哪。 沒有留痕的判斷沒辦法校準，而沒辦法校準的直覺不會變準，只會讓人越來越有自信。

Because three months later I want to be able to look up what I knew at the time, how I reasoned, and where I went wrong. Judgement that leaves no trace cannot be calibrated, and intuition that is never calibrated does not get sharper. It just gets more confident.

07

我怎麼跟自己吵架How I argue with myself

這是整套東西裡我最在意的部分。分析框架大家都有，會動手殺死自己框架的機制比較少見。 This is the part I care about most. Everyone has an analytical framework. Mechanisms that will actually kill your own framework are rarer.

  1. 可證偽條件Falsification conditions

    每個論點都要寫下一句：什麼證據出現，我就認錯。還要附到期日。時間到就得裁決，不能假裝忘記。

    Every claim needs one sentence: what evidence would make me concede. With an expiry date attached. When the date arrives it gets ruled on. Quietly forgetting is not allowed.

  2. 前提存活稽核Do the premises still live

    定期回頭問：這個結論當初靠的前提，現在還站得住嗎？前提死了，結論就得跟著死，就算它還在賺錢。

    Go back regularly and ask whether the premises a conclusion originally rested on are still standing. If a premise dies, the conclusion dies with it, even while it is still making money.

  3. 信心校準Confidence calibration

    下判斷的時候記下自己有幾分把握，事後對照實際的命中率。目標不是提高勝率，是讓把握跟結果對得起來。

    Record how sure I felt at the time, then compare it against what actually happened. The goal is not a higher hit rate. It is making confidence and outcome line up.

  4. 情報缺口清單A list of what I do not know

    把「我不知道什麼」明明白白寫下來，還給它期限。沒被登記的未知，很容易被當成已知。

    Write down what I do not know, explicitly, with a deadline on each item. An unknown that never gets logged quietly starts being treated as known.

  5. 矛盾信箱The contradiction inbox

    主動去找跟自己相反的證據，而不是等它自己撞上門。反證要有專屬的收件位置，不然它會被順手忽略。

    Go looking for evidence against myself instead of waiting for it to knock. Counter-evidence needs its own inbox, otherwise it gets brushed aside without anyone deciding to.

  6. 退化守衛Degeneration guard

    借自 Lakatos。一個框架如果每次被打臉都只能靠打補丁活下來，它已經在退化了。所以系統裡有規則專門偵測這種補丁，也預先封掉幾條我最愛用的脫罪路徑。

    Borrowed from Lakatos. If a framework survives every contradiction only by adding a patch, it has already begun to degenerate. So the system has rules that watch for those patches, and closes off a few of my favourite escape routes in advance.

  7. 陌生人測試The stranger test

    借自 Soros。定期問自己：一個完全不認識這套系統的聰明人，會買單嗎？「整體覺得有用」不算，我得拿得出三條具體的理由。

    Borrowed from Soros. Ask periodically whether a smart person who knows nothing about this system would buy it. "It feels useful overall" does not count. I need three concrete reasons.

  8. 反諂媚規則No credit for agreeing with me

    某個外部意見剛好站在我這邊，不代表它更有份量。支持跟證據是兩回事。

    An outside opinion does not carry more weight because it happens to be on my side. Agreement and evidence are different things.

「如果連一個目標跟我一樣的聰明陌生人，都不需要這項機制，
那它到底是在保護我的組合，還是在保護我對這套框架的感情？」 "If a smart stranger with the same goals would not need this mechanism,
is it protecting the portfolio, or protecting how I feel about the framework?" 留在系統審查紀錄裡的一句from an internal review note

08

親職：同一套紀律，最放不下的一次 Parenting: the same discipline, on the thing I can least afford to get wrong

我有兩個孩子，走體制外。做這個決定用的推理跟投資一模一樣。但我對它的自我審查更嚴格，因為這一注輸了沒得重來。 I have two children and we chose to educate them outside the system. The reasoning is identical to how I invest. But I audit it far harder, because this is the bet you cannot take back.

立場一Position one

學歷是衍生品，不是資產A degree is a derivative, not an asset

學歷本身沒有內在價值。它的市價是一種衍生品，底層資產是「這個經濟體願意為這張紙多付多少錢」。 經濟擴張的時候它像看漲期權，收縮的時候它像不良資產：帳面價格還在，但沒有人接手。

A degree has no intrinsic value. Its market price is a derivative, and the underlying asset is how much premium an economy is willing to pay for that piece of paper. In an expansion it behaves like a call option. In a contraction it behaves like a bad loan: the book value is still there, but nobody will take it off you.

這切斷了一條大家從小聽到大的因果鏈：努力讀書，好學歷，好工作，好人生。 第二節斷掉了。而且不是因為誰不夠努力，是因為定價權從來就不在我們手上。

That severs a chain most of us grew up hearing: study hard, good degree, good job, good life. The second link has broken. Not because anyone failed to work hard, but because the pricing power was never ours to begin with.

立場二Position two

不押環境確定性，押反脆弱Bet on antifragility, not on a certain environment

沒有人能用過去的數據預測四年後的產業長什麼樣。某個科系四年前還被大力推薦， 四年後那批畢業生一畢業就撞上那個產業崩掉。這不是誰失手， 是「選對位置就安全」這個想法本身有破洞。

Nobody can predict what an industry looks like four years out from past data. A field of study gets enthusiastically recommended, and four years later that cohort graduates straight into its collapse. That is not one person's misjudgement. The hole is in the idea that picking the right position makes you safe.

所以我不去優化入學路徑。當底層資產的定價又不穩又測不準， 用確定的成本去對沖一個不確定的東西，這筆交易不划算。

So I do not try to optimise the admissions path. When the pricing of the underlying is both unstable and unforecastable, paying a certain cost to hedge an uncertain asset is a bad trade.

立場三Position three

內部計分卡優先於外部計分卡Inner scorecard before outer scorecard

外部計分卡的邏輯是「我的價值取決於我有沒有站在正確的位置上」。 一個習慣這樣看自己的孩子，長大以後會是各種焦慮販賣機最好的客人。 那些聲稱知道「什麼才是正確位置」的人，賣的就是這個。

The outer scorecard runs on one premise: my worth depends on whether I am standing in the right place. A child who learns to see themselves that way grows into the ideal customer for anyone selling anxiety. People who claim to know which position is the correct one are selling exactly this.

人與事爭，不與人爭。Compete with the problem, not with people.

立場四Position four

對兩層結構下注Betting on a two-tier structure

等孩子成年，我猜工作會分成兩層。一層是能掌握底層邏輯、能跟 AI 一起做研究的人， 需要嚴謹的因果推理、系統性的思維，和夠厚的知識底。 另一層是照護、水電、需要高度信任的現場工作，需要情緒調節、建立信任、身體技能和臨場判斷。

By the time they are grown, my guess is that work splits in two. One tier is people who grasp the underlying logic and can do research alongside AI, which needs rigorous causal reasoning, systems thinking and a thick base of knowledge. The other is care work, trades and high-trust work done in person, which needs emotional regulation, the ability to build trust, physical skill and judgement on the spot.

兩層有個共同點：都不靠一張文憑當信號。

The two tiers have one thing in common: neither runs on a diploma as its signal.

三件我寫在藍圖旁邊、提醒自己別忘記的事 Three things I keep written in the margin of my own blueprint

一個框架如果只收集支持自己的證據，它就已經開始退化了。所以下面是我對自己這條路線的反證登記。 A framework that only collects evidence in its own favour has already started to rot. So here is the counter-evidence I keep on file against my own choice.

我這條路也是衍生品My path is a derivative too

「能力密度」這條路的底層資產是「未來的市場真的會獎勵真本事勝過文憑信號」。 我賭的方向可能是對的，但結構上，這跟「賭選對科系就有出路」是同一種操作： 都是用今天的判斷，押一個我控制不了的定價機制。

The underlying asset beneath a capability-density path is the assumption that the future market really will reward genuine ability over a credential signal. My direction may well be right, but structurally this is the same move as betting on the right major: using today's judgement to wager on a pricing mechanism I do not control.

而且學歷有一個我沒辦法否認的好處：好讀。資本三秒鐘就能評估一張文憑。 我幫孩子建的東西，對未來的市場好不好讀？這題我到現在還沒有答案。

And a degree has one advantage I cannot argue away: legibility. Capital can assess a diploma in three seconds. Whatever I am building for my children, how legible will it be to the market they walk into? I still do not have an answer.

我們家有一層玻璃地板There is a glass floor under our family

經濟學家不叫這個教養，他們叫代際資源移轉，講白了就是繼承。 共學的費用、長途旅行的時間、不進體制的機會成本，全都是繼承的具體形式。 如果我不是一個有餘裕的爸爸，「激進的自主」不會是一種教養哲學， 它會變成生存壓力下的無奈版本。

Economists do not call this parenting. They call it intergenerational transfer, which in plain language is inheritance. The cost of a learning co-op, the time for long trips, the opportunity cost of staying out of the system: all of it is inheritance in concrete form. If I were not a father with slack in his life, radical autonomy would not be a philosophy. It would be what survival pressure forced on us.

這不表示這條路是錯的。這表示這條路得知道自己站在什麼地板上。 而且等孩子長大，他們要看得見那層地板。把成果全歸給自己的內驅力，就是對特權的沉默。

None of which makes the path wrong. It means the path has to know what floor it is standing on. And when the children are grown, they need to be able to see that floor. Crediting the outcome entirely to your own drive is how privilege stays quiet.

被設計出來的自發性，本身就該被懷疑Engineered spontaneity deserves suspicion

上一輩的父母「是」父母：在那裡、照顧、不太插手。 這一輩的父母「做」父母：規劃、投資、監督、優化。 我就是後者的標準樣本。我甚至為此建了一整套知識系統。

The previous generation of parents simply were parents: present, caring, not intervening much. This generation does parenting: planning, investing, supervising, optimising. I am a textbook case of the second kind. I built an entire knowledge system for it.

弔詭的地方在這裡：我的藍圖上寫著「激進的自主」、「讓世界自己發生在孩子身上」， 但這些理念是被一個高度工程化的父母系統規劃出來的。 把親職做成一個動詞、做到這種程度，本身就是這個結構的症狀，不是解方。

Here is the awkward part. My blueprint says radical autonomy, says let the world happen to the child. And those ideas were planned by a heavily engineered parental system. Turning parenting into a verb to this degree is a symptom of the structure, not a cure for it.

我太太的教養不需要透過任何框架運作，而她做的一點都不比我少。這件事我花了好一段時間才真的看懂。

My wife's parenting runs through no framework at all, and she does no less than I do. It took me quite a while to properly understand that.

現階段我實際在做的事What I am actually working on right now

孩子還小。這個階段我該做的不是把系統優化得更精緻，是好好認識他。 這裡有一條界線我得一直提醒自己：「認識孩子」跟「確認我對孩子的假設」，方向是反的。 我心裡那些既有的敘事，他是哪一型的學習者、他有什麼特質，全都有過度擬合的風險。 所以我要求自己記下原始的畫面，而不是我詮釋過的畫面。 奇怪的問題、反常的安靜、被我忽略掉的小動作，資訊量常常比那些亮眼的時刻高得多。

They are still small. What this stage asks of me is not a more refined system. It is getting to know him properly. And there is a line I have to keep reminding myself about: getting to know a child and confirming my assumptions about him point in opposite directions. The narratives already in my head, what kind of learner he is, what he is like, all carry a risk of overfitting. So I make myself record the raw scene rather than my reading of it. The odd question, the unusual silence, the small gesture I nearly missed: those usually carry more information than the impressive moments.

'pick the right parenting philosophy and you'll be safe'?" 留在教育筆記裡，還沒回答的一題 a question left unanswered in my education notes

我拿來測試自己的書Books I use to test myself

讀書筆記的用途不是找人同意我，是拿作者來撞撞看我的框架。 Reading notes are not for finding someone who agrees with me. They are for crashing an author into my framework and seeing what dents.

  • Vaclav Smil How the World Really Works 能量和物質的現實預算。我的物理層就錨在這裡。 The real budget of energy and matter. My physical layer is anchored here.
  • Carlota Perez Technological Revolutions and Financial Capital 安裝期和部署期的資本，性格完全不一樣。 Capital in the installation period and in the deployment period behave like different animals.
  • Davidson & Rees-Mogg The Sovereign Individual 我是站在反論的位置讀的，刻意把不同意的地方記下來。 I read this one from the opposing side, deliberately writing down where I disagree.
  • Morgan Housel 行為金融。我把它當成不同典範的外部證人：跨範式的獨立收斂，比同溫層的附和有力得多。 Behavioural finance, treated as a witness from a different paradigm. Independent convergence across paradigms is worth far more than agreement from inside your own.
  • Howard Marks 市場週期。用來定位情緒的鐘擺現在在哪。 Market cycles, used to locate where the sentiment pendulum currently sits.
  • Peter Turchin 結構人口學。政治不穩定的先行結構。 Structural demography, as the structure that runs ahead of political instability.
